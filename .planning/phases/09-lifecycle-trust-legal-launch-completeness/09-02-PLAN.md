# Phase 9 — Wave 2: Legal Pages & Trust Surface (Frontend)

## Objective

Make the legal surface consistent with the public shell and complete the LGPD intermediary notice: restructure `/privacidade` and `/termos-de-uso` to render as content inside `(public)/layout.tsx`, convert them to the project's design tokens and heading hierarchy, add pt-BR metadata, add the required "somos apenas meio de divulgação" notice, and link both pages from the footer. Verify — without changing — the "Vaga revisada" badge and the report flow.

## Purpose

Criterion 4 is the only success criterion with real missing content; criterion 5's UI already exists but has never been asserted inside a Phase 9 plan. This wave closes the legal/trust gap without touching backend behaviour or rewriting the user-authored legal copy.
